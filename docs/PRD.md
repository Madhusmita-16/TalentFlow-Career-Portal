# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
**TalentFlow Career Portal** — Candidate Applicant Tracking System (ATS) & Job Board Platform

## Problem
Recruiters struggle with manual candidate application stage tracking, while job applicants lack dynamic job matching dashboards and application status updates.

## Target Users
- Job Seekers, Candidate Applicants, Recruiters, and Hiring Managers.

## Core Features
1. **Candidate Job Search & Easy Apply**: Searchable job board with filters and 1-click candidate apply modal.
2. **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)**: Recruiter dashboard for stage tracking (Applied, Reviewing, Interviewing, Offered, Rejected).
3. **Networking & Feed**: Professional social feed for career updates and articles.
4. **Candidate Profile Manager**: Work experience timeline and skill badge manager.
5. **AI Career Assistant Chat Widget**: Embedded AI chatbot offering interview prep guidance.

## Out of Scope
- Recruiter billing & payroll processing.
